1 · L-band & the blank

Why L-band sees water the official map can't

The official flood map uses a 5.6 cm C-band radar. Under forest it marks the area "cannot see". NISAR's 24 cm L-band wave goes through the leaves.

Rough ground scatterssome of the wave returns.
Open water is darka smooth surface sends the wave away. HH and HV both drop.
Flooded forest is brightwater + trunk form a corner reflector. HH rises, HV stays.

What we count

Only NISAR water inside the official map's blank. Water both maps see is not counted as ours.

adds = NISAR water ∩ GFM "cannot see" ∩ both valid

area = pixels × 80 m × 80 m

more = adds ÷ GFM flood

GFM = Copernicus Global Flood Monitoring (Sentinel-1). Nearest map within ±2 days, put on our 80 m grid.

"More %" is shown only where GFM maps ≥ 5 km² of flood, so tiny bases don't inflate it.

4 · Algorithm

From radar features to water

Each pixel vs its own dry self, not a fixed colour scale.

Backscatter in dBσdB=10log10σ0
Change vs its dry selfΔHHt=HHt−HH‾dry−dt
Drift of stable landdt=medianquiet 30%(HHt)

① Open water

HHt<tHH∧HVt<tHV (tHH*,tHV*)=arg maxtF1(NISAR,DSWx clear)

Mirror: both channels dark. Default −17 / −24 dB; tuned per site on cloud-free days.

② Water spreads

ΔHH≤−2.5∧ΔHV≤−2.5∧dist(open water)≤1 km

−2.5 dB ≈ half the power, ≫ 0.5 dB noise of stable land.

③ Flooded field

A≥0.5 km²∧HH‾≤−12∧ΔHH≤ΔHV+1

Paddies: big, dark, mirror-like (HH falls with HV, unlike harvest).

④ Water under trees

ΔHH≥+3∧|ΔHV|<1.5∧dist(water)≤3 km

Double bounce: +3 dB = power ×2; HV steady = canopy unchanged.

⑤ Permanent water

1T∑t=1T𝟙[HHt<−13∧HVt<−19]≥0.9

Lake core, river, sea: removed, never "flood".

km²=N pixels×0.08×0.08

7 · Limits

What we don't claim

Lower boundsShallow water in grass barely changes L-band. Read every number as "at least".
Candidates, not proofWater in the blank is a strong candidate. Flooded forest has no ground truth yet.
A sampleWettest pass per site, sites we picked. Not a global or average figure.
Provisional dataMost NISAR passes are still Provisional; calibration may change.
80 m gridGFM (20 m) and NISAR dates differ by ≤ 2 days. Small or fast floods blur.
Detector versionsSite numbers use detector v2. v3 (with coherence) is tested on one site so far.
